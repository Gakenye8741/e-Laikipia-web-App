**SECURE-VOTE** is a secure, transparent, and decentralized e-voting platform engineered for **Laikipia University** and higher education institutions. Designed to eliminate long voting queues, counting errors, and missed votes, SECURE-VOTE enables students to vote safely from their mobile devices with immutable, cryptographic verification.

## 🌟 Key Features

- **🔒 Immutable Blockchain Ledger:** Every vote is permanently anchored to smart contracts on the Ethereum Sepolia network, preventing administrative overrides, deletion, or record tampering.
- **📱 Vote from Anywhere:** Mobile voter application (React Native & Expo) paired with a high-contrast web dashboard allowing remote balloting.
- **📊 Real-Time Election Analytics:** Live turnout tracking, candidate tallies, coalition standings, and position results updated in real time.
- **🔑 Cryptographic Ballot Secrecy:** Voter payloads and identity tokens are secured using AES-256 encryption.
- **🔍 Self-Audit & Receipts:** Every student receives a cryptographic receipt hash to independently verify that their vote was recorded in the final tally.
